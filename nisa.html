For You, Baby

Dear Hanisah,

I don't always know how to put everything I feel into words, but I wanted to make something just for you because you mean so much to me.

I know I'm not perfect. Sometimes I don't express myself enough, sometimes I keep things inside, and sometimes the way I act doesn't show what I actually feel. I'm still learning how to communicate better and how to show the people I care about that they really matter to me.

But one thing I want you to know is that I appreciate having you in my life. I appreciate the little conversations, the laughs, the random moments, and even the moments where we don't agree. Every part of our time together has meant something to me.

You're not just someone I call "baby" or "sayang". You're someone whose presence has become important to me. I care about how you feel, what you dream about, and where life takes you.

I want to keep growing, not just for myself, but also in the way I treat and communicate with the people I love. I want my actions to speak louder than just saying "I care."

I know there will be good days and bad days. There will be moments when we understand each other perfectly and moments when we don't. But I hope we can always learn from those moments instead of letting them define us.

So if you ever wonder whether you matter to me, I hope this little page reminds you that you do.

Thank you for being you, Hanisah. ❤️

— For my baby
